Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MDJ Digital Solutions (“we”, “us”, “our”), based in Kolkata, India, collects, uses and protects information through our Meta app “MDj Automation Solution” and related messaging automation services for Facebook Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp (together, the “Service”).

We provide the Service to businesses. When you send a message or comment to a business that uses our Service, we process your information on that business’s behalf so it can reply to you.

1. What we collect

When you interact with a business that uses our Service, we may collect:

  • Message and comment text you send to the business on Messenger, Instagram or WhatsApp.
  • Platform user IDs (page-scoped or app-scoped IDs assigned by Meta) and, for WhatsApp, your phone number.
  • Usernames or profile names made available by the platform.
  • Timestamps of messages and comments.
  • Business access tokens granted by the businesses that connect their Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp number to our Service.
  • Contact details you choose to share in chat, such as your name, phone number, email address or delivery address.

We do not collect your Facebook or Instagram password, and we do not access any messages outside the conversations with the connected business.

2. How we use it

  • To receive your messages and comments and send replies on behalf of the business you contacted.
  • To generate AI-assisted replies using Google Gemini. Only the text of the message is sent to Gemini to produce a reply — not your user ID, username or other profile data. This data is not used to train AI models.
  • To keep conversation context so replies are relevant, and to let the business follow up on your enquiry.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and secure the Service.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not build marketing profiles.

3. Sharing

We share information only as follows:

  • The business you messaged — it can see your conversation and any details you share.
  • Service providers (processors) that help us run the Service:
    • n8n Cloud — workflow automation that routes messages and replies.
    • MongoDB Atlas — database storage of conversation data.
    • Google (Gemini API) — generation of AI-assisted reply text.
  • Legal requests — where required by law, court order or a valid request from a government authority.

We never sell your personal information.

4. Storage & security

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access tokens and conversation data are stored with access limited to people who need it to operate the Service.
  • We retain conversation data for up to 12 months from the last interaction, after which it is deleted. Data may be deleted sooner on request (see Data deletion) or when a business disconnects from the Service.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and request a copy.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Withdraw consent by stopping messaging the business, or by removing the app from your account settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at ju0868732@gmail.com.

6. Data deletion

You can ask us to delete all data associated with your Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp account at any time. Step-by-step instructions are on our User Data Deletion Instructions page.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated through the businesses using our Service where appropriate.
