User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The Meta app “MDj Automation Solution”, operated by MDJ Digital Solutions, stores limited data to reply to messages and comments you send to businesses using our Service (see our Privacy Policy). You can ask us to delete this data at any time.

Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to ju0868732@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line “Data deletion request”.
  3. Include your Instagram and/or Facebook username (and your WhatsApp number, if relevant) so we can find your data.

Email a deletion request

We will delete your messages, comments, user IDs, usernames and any contact details you shared within 7 working days, and send you an email confirmation once deletion is complete.

Remove the app from your account

You can also remove “MDj Automation Solution” from your account at any time. This stops the app from accessing your information in future. To also delete data we already hold, send the email request above.

Facebook

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Select Apps and websites (under “Your activity” / “Permissions”).
  3. Find MDj Automation Solution and select Remove.

Instagram

  1. Open Instagram and go to your profile → menu → Settings and activity.
  2. Select Website permissions → Apps and websites.
  3. Under Active, find MDj Automation Solution and select Remove.

Questions

If you have any questions about your data, contact us at ju0868732@gmail.com.
